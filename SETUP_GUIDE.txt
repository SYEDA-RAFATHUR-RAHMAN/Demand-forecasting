Q: What is the problem you are solving?
A: Retail stores lose money from overstock (too much stock) and
   understock (too little stock). Our system predicts future demand
   so managers know how much to order.

Q: What models did you use?
A: Four models:
   1. Moving Average — average of last 3 months (baseline)
   2. Linear Regression — finds trend in data (scikit-learn)
   3. Random Forest — 100 decision trees using lag + season features
   4. Prophet — Facebook/Meta's time-series model, handles
      trend + seasonality automatically. This is our main model.

Q: How do you evaluate the models?
A: MAE (Mean Absolute Error) — average units wrong per prediction
   RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error) — penalizes large errors more
   Lower value = more accurate model.

Q: What is Reorder Point?
A: ROP = Avg Daily Demand × Lead Time
   When stock falls to this level, place a new order.

Q: What is Safety Stock?
A: SS = Z × StdDev(demand) × √Lead Time
   Extra buffer stock to handle unexpected demand spikes.
   Z = 1.65 for 95% service level.

Q: Why Streamlit?
A: Streamlit converts Python code into a web app automatically.
   No HTML/CSS/JavaScript needed. Used in industry for data dashboards.

Q: Why Prophet over other models?
A: Prophet handles trend + seasonality automatically, gives
   confidence intervals, is interpretable, and is used by
   Meta (Facebook) and Amazon in production systems.
